Who I am

Nicolò, 30yo designer based in Milan.

Since 2024 I'm working as visual and UI designer at multidisciplinar design studio Zetalab.

From 2019 to 2024 I have been working for award-winning creative studio We Are Muesli, where I had the opportunity to work at both commercial and applied projects, mostly developed for cultural and artistic contexts.

Between 2020 and 2021 I was an academic tutor for the Game Design department at SAE Institute Milano, where I experienced working in the education field, both inside and outside of the classroom.

What I do

I am a passionate and versatile designer, and I create visual identities for digital and printed projects, trying to find new, compelling ways of telling stories and convey moods through images and layouts.

I design UIs and interactive experiences, applying user experience and game design concepts to digital interfaces and physical installations.

I create video games as a means of artistic expression, as they allow me to experiment with game design, writing, illustration, animation and coding. In 2023 I founded the video game collective Villadelfina with friend and fellow visual artist Alessandro Ferrari and released our first video game.
