Hómnia
-

A branding system for an innovative network of architects devoted to sustainability in building.

Art direction

Branding

Web design

Hómnia is a collective of highly trained architects, engineers and builders specialized in the development of eco-friendly housing projects. At Zetalab, we developed a visual system both for their non-profit organization and for its commercial counterpart, Hómnia Network.

I was in charge of this project from start to finish, from brand and logo design to printed brochures and their website.

First thing, we visually explored three different approaches to the Hómnia mood: Space and future, Impact and materials, and Personal wellbeing. After that, we designed three logo proposals.

Each proposal had a logo, a color palette and a possible visual application.

The third proposal was accepted, so we created the brand manual and all the applications ("certified" stamp, printed brochure, website, and social media profiles).
